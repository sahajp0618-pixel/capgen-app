About · Application Notes

From four numbers
to a finished cap.

CapGen is a parametric generator for the small protective and connector caps that electronics assembly lines consume by the thousand — produced on demand, in-house, without a single mould.

01 — Background

The idea

CapGen started as an internal tool at Reliance Electronics. Engineers kept needing one-off protective caps — for a connector, an exposed pin header, a trimmer, a port — and kept waiting days on the machine shop or on injection-moulded parts. The shortcut was to describe a cap by its dimensions instead of drawing it, and let the geometry build itself. Two families cover almost every case: a rounded rectangular box cap, and a closed-top cylinder cap with an external grip ring.

02 — Pipeline

How it works

You enter the outside dimensions and a press-fit tolerance. CapGen builds the mesh procedurally in the browser, then runs a repair-and-validation pass — welding vertices, normalising winding, closing boundary loops — and scores the result for printability. Only a watertight, certified mesh unlocks export. The cylinder cap is a direct translation of a proven OpenSCAD module, so the printed part matches the reference exactly. Everything runs client-side on Three.js — no server, nothing to install.

03 Where it fits — PCB & electronics manufacturing

Connector & port caps

Seal USB, RJ45, headers, and edge connectors against dust and ESD through storage, transit, and the line.

Component protectors

Shield potentiometers, crystals, relays, and tall parts from knocks during handling, test, and rework.

Masking for finishing

Plug ports and cover connectors through wave soldering, conformal coating, and aqueous cleaning.

Standoffs & jigs

Quick spacers, alignment fixtures, and locators sized to a specific board revision.

Shipping & field caps

Protective end-caps for cabled assemblies, enclosures, and serviceable ports in the field.

Prototyping

A matching cap the same day a new connector lands on the board — no tooling wait.

No toolingInjection moulds run into the thousands and take weeks to cut. A printed cap needs none — the geometry is the tool.
No minimum orderMake one, or a hundred, exactly when you need them. Inventory becomes a file, not a shelf.
ParametricAny size in seconds, so a board revision or a new connector never orphans a batch of caps.
Certified first printThe watertight check catches un-printable geometry before it wastes filament and machine time.
